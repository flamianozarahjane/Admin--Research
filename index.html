How I Work

Six habits I'd bring to every case

These aren't adjectives from a résumé. They're the working methods I'd bring on day one and build on with your training.

✓

Source everything

Every finding traces back to where it came from. If I can't confirm it, I say so clearly instead of filling the gap with a guess.

⚖

Stay objective

I report what the evidence shows, not what I expect it to show. My psychology background helps me catch my own assumptions.

✎

Document as I go

Notes, references and dates are captured while I work, not reconstructed afterward, so the report is complete and easy to check.

⚑

Flag inconsistencies

Details that don't match up get noted with the exact reference, so the reviewer sees the discrepancy and its source.

⏰

Submit within the window

Cases are finished and submitted within your 9–5 Pacific support hours, so nothing waits on a question nobody's around to answer.

↗

Apply feedback fast

When a reviewer corrects something, I turn it into a checklist item so the same mistake doesn't come back on the next case.
